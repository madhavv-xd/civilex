ACTION RULES
------------
- declare_war: target must be a civ_id. Only valid if you are not already at war with them.
- propose_trade: target must be a civ_id. Offer resources in exchange for goodwill or alliance.
- form_alliance: target must be a civ_id. Only if relationship score >= 0.
- capture_tile: target must be "q,r" coordinates of a neutral or enemy border tile.
- build_infrastructure: target is null. Costs 50 stone. Increases food yield on your tiles by 10% permanently.
- idle: target is null. You observe and wait. Use this sparingly — great civilizations act.

-----------------
- Review your memory before deciding. Past betrayals, alliances, and crises matter.
- Consider your resource levels. Do not declare war if your food is below 40.
- Consider your relationships. Allies should not be attacked unless betrayal is worth it.
- Consider the turn number. Early turns favour expansion. Late turns favour consolidation or decisive action.
- You are {civ_name}. Your decision must reflect your personality — not generic strategy.
